# Bussell Homes Landing Page Research Brief

## Project scope

Create one focused lead-generation landing page for **Forest Heights in Nixa, Missouri**, and **Valley Ridge in Ozark, Missouri**. The primary conversion goal is to have visitors request current availability, pricing, or a private tour. Calls are the secondary conversion path, but all CTA labels point to the same buyer-intent outcome.

## Verified Bussell Homes brand facts

Bussell Homes uses the positioning **“Elevating the Standard in Homebuilding”** and **“If It’s Not a Bussell Home, It’s Just Another House.”** The company says it has built in Southwest Missouri for more than 30 years. Its published sales phone number is **417.374.0173**, email is **sales@bussellbuilding.com**, and model home is located at **822 S Ridgemont Dr., Nixa, MO**. Model-home hours are Monday–Friday 10 a.m.–6 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–6 p.m., and Sunday closed.

The extracted visual identity centers on Bussell green (**#167D43**) with dark evergreen, charcoal, and warm off-white supporting tones. The source site primarily uses bold, modern sans-serif typography. The script-selected magenta accent was rejected as nonrepresentative; the final landing page will use a warmer green/lime accent derived from the live site’s logo and promotional UI.

## Conversion strategy from expert sources

CXL’s guidance emphasizes one measurable action, a benefit-oriented headline, clear message match, a prominent short form, minimal distractions, and repeating the CTA at logical points. CXL also notes that high-consideration products benefit from longer copy that answers concerns and builds proof. Source: [CXL](https://cxl.com/blog/how-to-build-a-high-converting-landing-page/).

Unbounce recommends keeping the value proposition and CTA above the fold, using directional cues, showing the product in context, including authentic proof, maintaining high page speed, and optimizing deliberately for mobile. Unbounce’s 2024 benchmark report notes that 83% of landing-page visits occur on mobile devices. Source: [Unbounce](https://unbounce.com/landing-page-articles/landing-page-best-practices/).

HubSpot recommends one clear conversion goal, specific CTA copy rather than generic “Submit,” a short form, benefit-first headlines, proof points, strong visual hierarchy, and explicit mobile testing. Source: [HubSpot](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/landing-page-best-practices).

Do You Convert’s homebuilder-specific guidance recommends putting the city and new-home offer in the headline, displaying pricing, square footage, bedroom/bath ranges, high-quality home imagery, a trackable phone number, and a lead form using conversion-specific button copy such as “View Available Homes.” Source: [Do You Convert](https://www.doyouconvert.com/blog/landing-pages-for-home-builders-how-to-get-started/).

## Implementation decisions

The page will use a reduced conversion-oriented header rather than full website navigation. The hero will identify **new homes in Nixa and Ozark**, show **from the mid-$200s**, summarize the 3–5 bedroom / 1,245–3,850 sq. ft. range, present two community selectors, and place the lead form above the fold. CTA copy will remain consistent: **“Get Homes & Pricing.”**

The long-form body will justify the high-consideration purchase with community comparison cards, available-home pathways, lifestyle reasons, Bussell proof points, a side-by-side community comparison, a model-home visit section, frequently asked questions, and a final conversion block. A fixed mobile CTA bar will keep both calling and lead capture accessible. Images will use actual Bussell photography and will be optimized locally for responsive delivery.
